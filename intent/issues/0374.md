`intent wb migrate <node>` carries every hand-authored inbox entry as a LIVE message, so a migrated inbox can be born over the store's 20-message bound, and from then on the SENDER is refused (`wb ask` fails) until the RECIPIENT clears. Found on Laksa's cutover (2026-09-14, laksa-vc, on the pair at d9703ba8f): four inboxes were born over the bound (hv from vc 98, ic from vc 67, vc from ic 40, vc from cc 28), the first `wb ask hv` after migration was refused, and with the ic node offline vc could not write to ic at all.

The bound is backpressure on a live conversation; a migration is not a conversation. Candidates, none ruled: carry entries older than the board's own heartbeat as handled; exempt migrated rows from the bound until the recipient's first clear; or have migrate report the over-bound inboxes and tell the human to clear them as the recipient before any node writes. The verbatim pre-migration copies each recipient keeps in `.history/` mean nothing is lost either way.

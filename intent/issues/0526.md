Seen (laksa-vc, 2026-09-23, upgrading Laksa to the installed 3.2.0 build): the done gate refused `intent wp done` on three finished work packages under completed threads that predate acceptance criteria, with "the thread has zero acceptance criteria (empty contract). Define ACs with `intent ac new`, or cancel the unit with `intent st cancel` or `intent wp cancel`." (`native/rust/crates/intentsvcs/src/contract.rs:596`). laksa-vc closed them by adding one non-test criterion each, satisfying it by named evidence (`intent ac satisfy --evidence <ref> <STID> <ACID>`), and then running `wp done`.

The remedy names half the path. A criterion added with `ac new` is unsatisfied, so following the remedy leads straight to a second refusal. For historical work, satisfying the new criterion by named evidence is the step that makes the close honest, and the remedy never names it.

Expected: the refusal names the whole path. Add a criterion with `intent ac new`, then satisfy it with `intent ac satisfy --evidence <ref> <STID> <ACID>` (or prove a test criterion green), or cancel the unit.

It is XS and a 3.2.1 item. Routing (vc): ic, as remedy wording; vc judges.

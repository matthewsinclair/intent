## The fix (S), banked by cc at refs/bank/cc/pipefail/patch

`scan_file` becomes one `awk` per file applying the same ERE: the first-character comment rule, `PAT`, the `path:lineno:stripped` output, the population assertion and the two-sided control are unchanged, and the control drives the new scanner. Measured 28.10 s to 0.29 s for the arm. One trap recorded in the bank's note: `awk -v` interprets backslash escapes, so a `PAT` that grows a backslash is caught by the planted-defect control as CONTROL FAILED; `ENVIRON` is the escape-free door if ever needed.

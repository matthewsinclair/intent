`intent wb migrate <node>` writes the store and the index and no views, and neither its report nor any remedy names the step that should. After a migrate, `board.json` still holds the empty row register rendered and `wip.md` still holds the hand-authored bytes, so `intent doctor` reports view-skew (Gtools: vc/wip.md "7065 bytes on disk, 448 rendered") and the pre-commit doctor gate refuses every commit on the machine, other sessions' included, until some later board write happens to re-render. Found in Gtools (gtools-vc, 2026-09-14); repaired there at c930daf.

Cause, read against the source at v3.0.2 (e75708756): `Facade::wb_migrate` ends with `self.reindex_boards()` under the comment "Index only: the board this read stays on disk until the regeneration", and the doc on `land_board_write` says "a migrated board's views come from the regeneration that follows the migration". Nothing follows it: `report_wb_migration` prints the carried and uncarried lines and no next step.

The index-only rule is right while migrate is reading, since rendering first would overwrite its input. Once every file is read and the carry is written, nothing is left to protect. Fix: migrate lands its own board's views after the carry, so its report and the tree agree.

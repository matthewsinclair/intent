## What happens

A node builds each unit in a linked worktree under `tmp/`, banks it, and the unit lands on main from the bank. Nothing in the fold text says the worktree is finished with once its unit has landed, so it stays on disk with its own build target. vc's read-only inventory of 2026-10-26 (`refs/bank/vc/tools/wt-inventory.sh` and `wt-pass2.sh`) found stale worktrees from every authoring node. Each owner confirmed its own as stale, and their build targets held roughly 139 GB.

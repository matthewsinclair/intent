`intent wb migrate <node>` drops any text an inbox entry carries in its heading, so an entry whose text sat in the `## (...)` line with nothing below it is carried with an empty body, and the text is reported nowhere. Found on Laksa's cutover (laksa-ic, 2026-09-14).

Read against the source at v3.0.2 (e75708756): `wbmigrate::read_inbox` splits the heading at the first `)`, reads only `Re:` and `FYI only` out of what follows, and discards the rest; the body is only the lines below the heading. The module's own rule is that a drop with no record is indistinguishable from a line that was never there, and every other unread line goes to `uncarried`.

Fix: text in the heading after the stamp that is neither the `Re:` field nor the FYI marker is carried as the body's first line (or the body, when there is none below), or else reported as uncarried with its line number. It is never discarded silently.

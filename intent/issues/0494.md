An event's `ts` is taken when the write got through rather than when the act was recorded, so ordering by it across a contended burst gives the wrong order.

**Seen 2026-09-19** in Lamplight, in the same `database is locked` burst that produced 0487, and split out of it because it is a defect in what a stamp MEANS rather than in a remedy's wording. cc's `wb clear` event carries `ts` 15:11:36.868Z, while Lamplight commit ceab7404a -- made at 15:11:23Z, thirteen seconds EARLIER -- already contains that clear's render: `cc/inbox.vc.md` shows the entry as `(handled)`. Both of cc's writes had waited on the store lock.

**So the stamp is taken after the contention rather than before it.** An event whose effect is already committed carries a time later than the commit that holds it, which is not a rounding difference: it is the length of the wait, and the wait is bounded at five seconds per connection (`store.rs`, `BUSY_TIMEOUT_MS`), so the error can be seconds and can differ per event in one burst.

**Why it matters beyond being untidy.** The event log is the one artefact nothing recomputes. Anything that reads it back in order -- a reconstruction, an audit of who did what first, a question about whether A happened before B -- gets an order that is not the order the acts happened in, and gets it silently, because every stamp is individually plausible. Under contention the events of a burst can be reordered relative to each other and relative to the commits that carry their effects. Nothing reports this; the log looks correct.

**The ask:** an event's `ts` is read at the moment the act is RECORDED, before the write contends for the store, so the stamp says when the thing happened rather than when the writer got its turn. Where that is not possible, the event carries both and the log says which is which. Whether the existing stamps can be corrected is a separate question and this does not ask for it.

**Not closed by 0487.** 0487 (b) landed a board-specific remedy and nothing in it touches the stamp. 0487 (a) was withdrawn as forbidden by `store.rs`'s "refuse and name, never retry" ruling, which leaves the contention wait in place and therefore leaves this defect exactly as it was. S.

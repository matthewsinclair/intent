`intent index status` reports no index sizes, though the design and AC-19.6 commit it to the measured sizes.

ST0069's design (Data model additions, Risks) makes index size load-bearing once source is indexed (D34: FTS roughly twice its corpus) and says `intent index status` reports the measured sizes rather than anyone estimating them; AC-19.6 says the verbs report measured sizes.

The built `Status` (`intentsvcs/src/index/status.rs:22-36`) carries files held per corpus, grammar readiness per declared language, and skipped paths by reason, and `report_index` renders those (`intent-cli/src/render.rs:4340-4380`). Nothing reads the size of `src_sections`, `doc_sections`, `symbols` or the store, so no surface measures what the index costs.

Remedy: report the measured size of the index's tables, read and never estimated (eg SQLite's `dbstat` or page counts); or rule the claim out of the design and AC-19.6.

CI run 45328303838 (3.5.2 candidate at 029297dee) failed the_daemon_harness_can_restart::a_restart_replaces_the_process_and_keeps_the_address at tests/common/mod.rs:590, on macos-latest. The same assertion failed on ubuntu in run 45326139850.

The assertion was `children_of(self.pid()).is_empty()`, meant as a witness that `intent daemon run` execs into intentd. intentd spawns `census::tick()` at start (intentd/src/main.rs), which is built on `tokio::time::interval`, whose first tick completes at once. Its first round runs `census::sample`, which runs `ps -axo pid=,args=` (on macOS also `sysctl` and `lsof`, and `host_prune` runs `ps`). Each of those is a direct child of the intentd pid. A pgrep that lands while one is alive fails the check, though the exec happened.

daemon_run_execs.rs held the same "no children" arm.

Fix (test-only): the harness asserts `image_of(pid()) == "intentd"`, the claim the children check stood in for (a spawn leaves `intent` at that pid). daemon_run_execs asserts no child whose image is intentd, and `image_of` and `children_of` have one home in tests/common.


Resolution: fixed by f3a168417 on main, and shipped in tag v3.5.2 at fabbffd8c. Every leg of candidate run 45371863729 was green (local, macos and kovacs).

The search envelope never carries `reconciled_at`, and nothing records when the index was last reconciled.

ST0069's design makes the last reconcile part of every answer's freshness block: the Architecture table has `reconcile` stamping `index_state` and `freshness` reading it, the envelope example carries `"reconciled_at"`, and the Data model lists an `index_state` table. `search.rs` says the field stays `None` only until WP-18's reconcile exists to write it.

WP-18 is done and its reconcile (`Facade::index_rebuild`, `Facade::index_refresh`) stamps nothing. No `index_state` table or column exists, and every `IndexFreshness` is built through `new` (`intentsvcs/src/search.rs:158-165`), which sets `reconciled_at` to `None`, so the serialiser omits the field from every envelope.

It matters because a daemon-served search does not reconcile before answering (`intentd/src/store.rs:896-909`): the envelope is the only place a reader could learn how old that answer is.

Remedy: stamp the reconcile in the statement that performs it (D42, no clock read at render) and read it into `reconciled_at`; or rule the field and the `index_state` row out of the design.

Found by the as-written against as-built audit of ST0069, 2026-09-13.

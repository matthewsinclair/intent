THE FIX (S; hv, in ic's session on 2026-10-27, "Ok, do 1 and 2.", on ic's offer; vc's three points of 07:04Z by vc's clock):
- `usage::carry`: a failed read after a good one keeps that reading, still stamped with its `read_at`, with the failure named in `usage.error`. A failure with nothing good behind it stays the failure, and a good read replaces whatever was kept. `usage::resolve` shows a kept reading unless a statusline report is newer, and then it shows the report, naming the same failure.
- `usage::next_wait`: while the estate is asked for, the census reads the endpoint at most every five minutes (`READ_EVERY`). After a failure it waits twice the previous wait, from five minutes up to an hour (`WAIT_CAP`), and never sooner than a 429's `Retry-After` in seconds. A 429 is its own failure, `EndpointFailure::Throttled { retry_after }`, read from curl's `-w "\n%{http_code} %header{retry-after}"`; every other status stays `Status { status }`.
- census.rs keeps the schedule in the tick: an ask that arrives before the read is due stays armed rather than spent.

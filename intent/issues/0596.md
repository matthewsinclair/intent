**A FIRST v2 CONVERSION PRINTS "the views of undeclared threads were not examined: this project has not been migrated to Intent v3 -- it declares Intent 2.19.0" ON THE LINE BEFORE ITS `ok:`, AND THE DEHYDRATION THAT LINE NAMES NEVER RUNS ON A FIRST CONVERSION.** This is M2, which 3.2.2 shipped documented at docs/migrating-from-v2.md:18 ("the `ok:` line is the verdict").

**What was seen.** dc drove `intent upgrade` on the installed pair (701c4d98b) on 2026-10-14, in scratch projects under an isolated HOME, from upgrade_command.rs's own v2 fixture at 2.19.0, in four layouts: WIP flat only; WIP flat with a Completed thread in COMPLETED/; WIP flat with an On Hold thread flat; WIP flat with a Completed thread flat. Every first conversion printed the line, at exit 0. The changes to facade.rs since 701c4d98b are outside the upgrade path, so the drive speaks for HEAD.

**Mechanism (dc's reading; vc read the lines).** The prefix is written only by the Err arm at `native/rust/crates/intentsvcs/src/facade.rs:4565`. `Facade::upgrade` opens a facade with `Facade::open(project.clone(), ...)` at facade.rs:4554, on the Project discovered before the conversion. Its in-memory config still declares 2.19.0 while config.json on disk already reads the v3 version, so the open is refused as unmigrated, and issue 0316's dehydration of undeclared thread views does not run. On v2's ordinary layout this costs nothing: the prune removes the bucket files, doctor exits 0, and every later upgrade dehydrates. It does hide the refusal that the issue filed beside this one describes.

**Fix (XS).** Rediscover the Project from its root after the version stamp lands and before the facade opens, at that one site. One arm in upgrade_command.rs: a first conversion prints no "not examined" line, red at the base and green with the fix. The sentence at docs/migrating-from-v2.md:18 that tells the reader to read past the line is retired, and a CHANGELOG line goes under 3.2.4. It owes intentsvcs's and intent-cli's whole suites after `cargo build -p intentd`, and clippy.

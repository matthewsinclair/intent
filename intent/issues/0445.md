`~/.config/intent/projects.json` on the developer's machine holds `{"added_by": "discover", "root": "/private/var/folders/nn/p40vzghs67v8yq0p5y416yf80000gn/T/.tmpHoV7gx"}`, a directory that no longer exists. A test wrote into the user's REAL project registry.

## Mechanism (read from the code, not yet driven -- driving it writes the real registry again)

`native/rust/crates/intent-cli/tests/dual_path_conformance.rs` runs every shipped verb down two routes. `via_binary` sets `HOME` to a fixture; `via_library` calls `render::run` in-process and inherits the developer's real environment, which the file itself documents under `Hazard::ActsOnAmbientUserState`. Rows that reach ambient per-user state must be declared with that hazard. `daemon start|stop|restart` and `app start|stop|restart|status` are; **`discover` and `explore` are not**, and both write the registry through `intentsvcs::userstate::project_registry()` (`render.rs` `discover` at :5204, `explore` at :5397). So the in-process run of `discover` against the fixture's tempdir registers that tempdir for real.

## Evidence

- The entry is `added_by: discover`; `.tmpXXXXXX` is the Rust `tempfile` crate's naming, and the fixture is `tempfile::tempdir()`.
- hv's full suite of 2026-09-17 (`tmp/test/20260917-2128.RUST.out`, measured at `04cd00b47` clean): `dual_path_conformance` is the first integration binary the Rust half runs, at 22:31 local, the minute the entry was written.
- The same binary printed about 40 lines of the machine's REAL intentd log (`~/.local/state/intent/...`, stamps from 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-17) into the suite output: the in-process route is reading the real per-user state.

## Fix

Declare `discover` and `explore` as `Hazard::ActsOnAmbientUserState`, and find the row that printed the real daemon log and declare it too. The declaration is the fix, not a `HOME` for `via_library` (the file's own reasoning). Then prune the dead entry through the verb that owns the registry, before the fleet trawl reads it. XS.

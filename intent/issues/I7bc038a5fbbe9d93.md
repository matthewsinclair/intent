Raised by AE, through intent-vc, on 2026-11-24. Reproduced on Intent 3.5.2 (fabbffd8c).

## Problem

`intent ac new <st> AC-01.1 ...` on a thread with git-style packages refuses with the remedy "Group the label under the thread's `00` or one of its packages, eg `AC-81453070.1`; `intent wp list ST6d1f96f` shows the packages". `intent wp list` and `intent wp show` show each package's 4-hex shown form ("8145", "WP-8145"), not the 8-hex group that a label stores. So the remedy sends the reader to a list that does not hold the form it asks for.

Where: `FacadeError::LabelNamesAbsentPackage`'s remedy (intentsvcs/src/facade.rs).

## Fix

The remedy names each group the thread has, whole: `00` and each package's stored 8-hex id, so the reader does not need a second command. Owner cc, size XS.

## Related

AE also reports that `ac new <st> AC-7655.3` (a 4-hex package prefix) passes and is stored as `AC-76555923.3`. That is on purpose in the code: `Facade::whole_label` completes a package prefix in a label's group and stores it whole (design, "Criteria and tests"). hv decision 92 says labels take no prefix. intent-vc puts that to hv. This issue does not change it.

Resolution: Fixed in the b353 batch, landed on main at b15c70157. Released in v3.5.3 (tag v3.5.3 at 8475de825).

`intent wb migrate`'s closing line reports `N snapshot(s)`, and on two estates that number did not match the snapshot files the carry left on disk.

**Seen, on the Intent 3.2.0 pair (4e2f908a5), 2026-09-21:**

| estate   | node | reported       | files under `.history/pre-migration/` |
| -------- | ---- | -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| arca_cli | cc   | 3 snapshot(s)  | 1                                     |
| arca_cli | vc   | 3 snapshot(s)  | 1                                     |
| Conflab  | cc   | 27 snapshot(s) | 4                                     |
| Conflab  | dc   | 10 snapshot(s) | 3                                     |
| Conflab  | ic   | 21 snapshot(s) | 4                                     |
| Conflab  | vc   | 29 snapshot(s) | 1                                     |

The carries themselves are sound. Every unit dropped on `--drop-uncarried` is present in a pre-migration file, checked by byte comparison against a copy of each board taken before the carry. So nothing is missing. The number is what does not match. The carries are Conflab a0fc931c and arca_cli c99e21c.

**Expected:** a report line's number counts what its label names, or the label says what it counts. Whoever reads `3 snapshot(s)` beside one snapshot file cannot tell whether two are missing or the number counts something else. A carrier checking that its dropped units were kept reads this line to decide.

The ask is the report line only. What the number counts today is not established here.

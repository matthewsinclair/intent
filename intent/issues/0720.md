## What happens

The two commit-time gates read a guard's exit status with one meaning and two copies of it.

- `bin/.devbin/cmd/precommit`'s `tool_verdict` is the reading issue 0714 made the one: a guard runs as `x=0; bash <tool> || x=$?`, and then 0 passes, 1 is the finding its step names, and anything else is "gave no verdict (exit N)", named by its code and never by a cause it did not establish.
- `bin/.devbin/cut/gate`'s `check_contract` and `check_reference` each restate that three-way reading in their own `case` arms, with their own wording ("gave no verdict (rc=N)"), and report it through cut/gate's own `refuse()`.

Nothing is wrong today, but two copies of one meaning drift: a change to what a guard's status means reaches one gate and not the other.

## The fix

One primitive that both gates source maps a guard's status to its verdict (pass, finding, or no verdict with its code), and holds the "gave no verdict" wording once. precommit still adds a line to its report and cut/gate still refuses at once with its log and remedy, each through its own reporter, but neither restates the reading.

Found with issue 0714 (2026-11-19). It rides this release (hv decision 69).

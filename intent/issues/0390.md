Raised upstream on hv's ruling of 2026-09-14 (relayed by laksa-vc), filed by vc at 2026-09-14 17:03Z.

Laksa's `bin/hooks/guards/whiteboard-header-guard.sh` carries two checks. Check 1 refuses a YAML-escaped header value; it shipped upstream on 2026-08-16 as `lib/templates/hooks/whiteboard-header-guard.sh` with no harness. Check 2, added in Laksa on 2026-09-12, refuses a header value DETACHED from its key: a `focus:` value expanded over several lines by a formatter or a hand edit leaves the key with no value on its own line, and `fm_get` reads it as empty. Laksa met it three times on one board (430eda077 to bc66ddbc1, the value expanded over six lines; to 09e4819c5, still detached; to a0077c605, reattached). Upstream's guard has check 1 only.

The payload is Laksa's `bin/hooks/guards/whiteboard-header-guard.test.sh`: a throwaway-repo harness whose first three cases replay those commit pairs as real cases (two RED, the node's own repair a PASS the guard must not refuse), with check 1 re-proved after them, and every case asserting that something was staged before believing an exit code (a guard over an empty corpus returns 0 and is indistinguishable from a pass).

Fix: port check 2 into the shipped guard, and carry the harness with it, its real cases replaced by fixtures upstream can reach (or the harness reading its cases from the consumer that has them). Lane: dc.

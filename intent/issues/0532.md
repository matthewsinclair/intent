Found (ic, 2026-09-23, in the review of 0525's `(edited)` surface). The instrument was `pandoc -f gfm`, positive-controlled on the same item text before and after an edit. `views::wb_board_body` renders each live board item as `- {text}`, with the text inserted raw (views.rs, its item loop). An item's text can run to several lines, and its continuation lines are not indented under the `- `, so they leave the list item:

- **Sub-bullets render as sibling items.** Take an item whose text is `two steps:` followed by `- first` and `- second`. The view renders it as three items, and nothing marks the last two as lines of the first.
- **A fenced block inside an item can swallow the rest of the board.** This is live today. Conflab's `intent/whiteboard/dc/wip.md` carries 5 `## ` headings, and `pandoc -f gfm` reads only 2 of them (`DevX Claude (dc)` and `DOING`), because dc's doing 10 is a three-line fenced block. Its opening fence sits inside the list item. The unindented lines after it leave the item, and its closing fence, at column 0, then opens a new block that runs to the end of the file. The board's other sections and its footer all render as code.
- **`wb show` and `wb pickup` also print the continuation lines at column 0**, below the item's `[kind] seq` line, where they read as unattached text.

The raw markdown, which is how a node reads a board, is complete in every case. The damage is to the rendered page.

**Population.** Measured over every live item on every board under `~/Devel/prj/*/intent/whiteboard`, at 2026-09-23 12:24Z by date -u:

- 77 of 1,118 live items are multi-line.
- They sit on 14 boards across 7 estates: Prolix 49, Gtools 18, Conflab 3, Baize 2, Intent 2, Laksa 2 and Devbin 1.
- By their last line, 1 ends with a fence, 17 with a list item and 59 with prose.
- The population moves as boards change. An earlier count, between 12:13Z and 12:15Z, read 78 multi-line items, 18 of them ending with a list item.

0525's `(edited)` mark met the same class from the other side. Appended after an item's last line, it turned a closing fence into "``` (edited)", which CommonMark does not accept as a closing fence. That half is fixed inside 0525, which moves the mark to the end of the item's first line (vc's ruling, 2026-09-23). This issue is what remains, and no placement of a mark addresses it.

**Fix direction and blast radius** (vc, 2026-09-23; confirmed in the source by ic). Indenting each continuation line by two spaces keeps an item's lines inside its list item, and it is the whole fix for the view. The cost is in doctor:

- `views::skew` compares every generated view byte for byte against the render, board views included.
- Its only tolerance is text the renderer owns (`differs_only_in_renderer_owned_text`: the footer, the Acceptance paragraph it writes, and since 0528 the todo view's generator marker). Indentation is not renderer-owned text.
- So a fix that changes the render alone would turn every board view holding a multi-line item into a counted `ViewSkew`, in every estate on this machine, until that board is re-rendered.
- With `doctor` on the pre-commit gate (0308), the gate would then refuse commits in those estates.

That holds unless the fix also teaches doctor the old shape, as 0528 did for `todo.md`: a board view that differs from the render only in how item continuation lines are indented would then read as a stale render, not a skew. `wb show` and `wb pickup` write no file, so their half of the fix carries no such cost.

Timing: vc is taking it to hv with a recommendation. hv's rule for 3.2.1 is every outstanding item.

## What happens

When `cargo test --workspace` fails, `bin/.devbin/cut/gate`'s rust check (about :230) asks whether every red is in the load-sensitive daemon family with `! printf '%s\n' "$reds" | grep -v -E "$LOAD_SENSITIVE" | grep -q .`, under `set -o pipefail`. `grep -q .` exits at the first red outside the family. If the middle `grep -v` still has output to write, it takes SIGPIPE and exits 141, pipefail makes the pipeline's status 141, and `!` turns that into true. The gate then reads a red outside the family as confined to it, re-runs only intentd's tests, and a green re-run clears the step: a real red can pass the cut's own gate. A few reds fit in one write and escape the race; enough reds to fill the pipe's buffer meet it.

`intent/st/ST0056/parity/tools/pipefail_sigpipe_check.sh`, the guard for this class, does not see it: its population is the `.sh` files under its POP_DIRS, which do not include `bin/.devbin`, and `cut/gate` and `cmd/precommit` carry no extension.

## The fix

- `! grep -q -v -E "$LOAD_SENSITIVE" <<<"$reds"`: one process, no pipe.
- The guard's population reaches the gates' own shell: the bash files under `bin/.devbin/cut` and `bin/.devbin/cmd`, taken by their shebang rather than their extension. Whatever the wider guard then finds there is fixed or named.

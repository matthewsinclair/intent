`intent wb migrate` drops a board's lead paragraphs -- the prose above its first section -- and exits 0 regardless. Five on devbin's boards, each reported on stderr as `uncarried:` with the reason "the model has no field for it"; carry-map independently reads all five ABSENT. The report is correct and the exit status is not: a scripted migration cannot tell from rc that anything went. The bytes survive in devbin's `.history/20260916/` snapshots and in git at `24429c2`, compared with `cmp` against a cross-node red control. Devbin letter F; the drop measured by both, the exit status by devbin/vc.

Found by devbin migrating its boards on Intent 3.0.3 (`d24487079`), sent through intent/vc on hv's routing, and ruled by hv on 2026-09-16 to be fixed in this release (hv decision 18). The full measurement record, with the instrument (`carry-map.sh`, driven red and green) and who measured what, is devbin `1824c75`, `intent/whiteboard/dc/.history/20260916/wb-upstream-defects-draft.md`.

**Shared cause with the other migrate carry issues filed with this one (devbin letters A, B, D, E and F):** `wb_item` is `id, node, kind, seq, text, state, archived_at, recorded_at, authored_at, updated_at`, and has no field for a board lead, a section lead, a sub-heading, a group, a table or a heading qualifier; the section a line renders under is derived from `kind`. Whether the answer is a richer model or a migrator that never coerces and reports everything it cannot carry is ruled once for all five.

Fix: a migration that reports anything uncarried says so in its exit status (or states in its closing line, and in its register row, that rc 0 means "ran" and not "carried everything"), so a script can distinguish a complete carry from a lossy one.

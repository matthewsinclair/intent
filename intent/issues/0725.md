## What happens

Issue 0724 routed ten precommit steps through `run_guard`, which reads each guard by the kind its caller names (`guard_verdict <status> <kind>`). Three guards with more than two exit codes are still read two ways, `if ! bash "$TOOLS/<tool>"`:

- `thread_view_skew_check.sh` declares 0, 1 and 2 in its header. precommit reads it two ways, under a label that names both causes.
- `residue_class_check.sh`'s header says "Reports; never gates", but precommit's comment says its 1 blocks, and the step blocks on any non-zero. It also runs under `set -e`, so its 1 does not reliably mean "the sets differ". Its kind wants settling once.
- `runner_roster_check.sh` declares 0, 1 and 2. precommit's comment "THE ONE READING ... IS guard_verdict" stands beside ten `if !` steps, so the claim is wider than the code.

## The fix

Route each three-code guard through `run_guard` with its declared kind, and settle residue_class_check's kind once, in its header and in precommit. Then either every guard precommit runs goes through guard_verdict, or the comment says exactly which ones do.

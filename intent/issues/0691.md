hv, 2026-11-13, on the estate page's PLAN USAGE card, which read `usage endpoint failed: the endpoint answered 429, too many requests. No fallback has reported either.` with every bar empty (hv's screenshot, 10:26:06 local, 06:26:06Z): "Any reason this is blank". Then: "Ok, not for today. Please create an issue to resolve this post 3.3.1."

WHAT WAS SEEN. The installed build was `intent 3.3.0 (brew, 1e423060f919) release`, and its intentd had started at 06:24:43Z by `ps` lstart converted with `date -u`, about 80 seconds before the screenshot. `~/.local/state/intent/estate/` held no `*.usage.json`. 0658's fix is in that build: `usage::carry`, `usage::next_wait`, `READ_EVERY`, `WAIT_CAP` and `EndpointFailure::Throttled` are all in intentsvcs/src/estate/usage.rs.

WHY THE CARD IS EMPTY ANYWAY. 0658 made a failed read after a good one keep the good reading, but the kept reading lives only in intentd's memory. A restarted intentd has nothing to keep, so when its first read meets a 429 the card shows the failure with no numbers. That is the case `usage::carry` leaves as the failure, by design: "A failure with nothing good behind it stays the failure." The statusline fallback, which could fill the gap, has never reported on this host (0658's point 3: hv's statusLine does not pipe its input to `intent estate statusline`). The 429 itself is the account's: the endpoint throttles the account's token, which other readers share, eg hv's Claude Usage menubar app.

WHAT TO DECIDE AND FIX (post-3.3.1, on hv's word). Options, for vc to put to hv:
- Keep the last good reading across restarts: intentd writes it, with its `read_at`, under `~/.local/state/intent/estate/`, and seeds `carry` from it at start, so a restart that meets a 429 still shows the last numbers and their age. The card already draws a reading beside an error with its age (0658), so shell.html need not change.
- Honour the throttle across restarts too: keep the next-read-due time and the backoff state there as well, so a restart does not ask a refusing endpoint at once.
- Wire the statusline fallback on hv's machines (hv's settings.json, outside Intent's tree), or say on the card how to wire it.

**THE CRITIC SUBAGENTS INSTALLED IN A PROJECT CANNOT READ THE CONTRACT THEY WORK UNDER.** `intent/docs/critics.md` is the critic contract (the Critic Family section of the generated AGENTS.md names it), and it exists only in Intent's source tree. The brew keg carries no copy (checked on 3.3.3's, `/opt/homebrew/Cellar/intent/3.3.3/libexec`). No critic's `agent.md` under `intent/plugins/claude/subagents/` points at it. And `intent claude` serves the rules (`intent claude rules list` and `show`), not the contract. A critic running in another project therefore works to a contract it cannot open.

**History.** vc todo 65, from hv's decision 89 (2026-10-25, relayed by molt-vc): making the critic contract readable to installed subagents waited until after 3.2.7. It was not built by 3.3.3. hv ruled on 2026-11-17 (hv decision 59) that it rides the next release.

**What a fix has to do.** An installed critic reads its contract from the installed Intent, as it reads its rules: the contract keeps one home, `critics.md`, and whatever serves it reads that file, never a copy. Owner: ic, for the shape (how the installed tool serves the contract and how each critic's `agent.md` is pointed at it), with cc agreeing its meaning, since the contract is a rules document.

## What

`intent/docs/releasing.md` step 2 says "Two runs over an unchanged explorer write byte-identical files, so a clean status means the page still shows what ships." That holds only within one day. ic measured this on 2026-10-22 at the 3.2.5 cut's screenshot pass:

- `gen_explorer_shots.sh` ran on the pair at 8bef2bb7a (pyte 0.8.2), writing all 11 shots into scratch. 9 were byte-identical to `docs/images/explorer`. `edit.png` and `thread.png` differed, at the same 1758x1092 size.
- In both, the only change above a 5% difference threshold is one 31x22 px region (x 425..455, y 415..436). It is the demo thread's "created" field: 2026-10-16 in the committed shots, taken at the 3.2.4 cut-prep pass (ef9889423), against 2026-10-22. The rest of `edit.png`'s changed pixels are below 5%, which is the 64-colour palette reduction shifting.
- The cause is that the tool seeds its demo projects fresh under `/tmp/intent-demo` on every run, and the seeded thread is created on the day of the run. The tool elides the version and commit the explorer draws, but not dates.

So at every cut taken on a new day, two shots change while the explorer has not, and a clean `git status -- docs/images/explorer` cannot be the cut's test. At this cut the shots stay as committed, on vc's word.

## Expected

The tool pins the seed's date, so two runs over an unchanged explorer are byte-identical on any day and a clean status is the cut's test again. Not measured: whether the seed can set a thread's created date through a verb a reader could type. Failing that, `releasing.md` names the seed's date as the one expected difference.

Found by ic at the 3.2.5 cut's screenshot pass. Filed by ic on vc's word.

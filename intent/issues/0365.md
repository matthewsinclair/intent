Reported by courses-vc on e70c3528a, possibly by design (D33): a migrated message's rendered heading carries its `recorded_at`, not the time it was sent. The entry Courses' vc sent to cc at 14:51Z renders as `## (2026-09-13 15:22Z)` in `cc/inbox.vc.md` after the migration; the sent time survives only as `authored_at` in `cc/board.json`. A reader of the view can no longer tell when a message was sent, and the heading is the anchor replies thread against, so a reply written before the migration now cites a stamp the view does not show. If D33 intends the heading to be the recorded time, the view should carry the authored time beside it; if not, the heading should render `authored_at`.

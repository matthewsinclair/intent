The release driver's stamp step runs `"$STAMP_BIN" sync --to-disk` in-process (`bin/.devbin/cmd/build.d/release:1149`) while intentd may be watching the project, and `sync` refuses beside a watching daemon (exit 2, since c75a5b1a6). During the 3.1.0 cut hv had to stop the daemon by hand for this step.

Fix: the driver stops or pauses intentd for the stamp and restarts it afterwards, or asks the daemon to render once the pair is rebuilt. S. vc's next-line list, item 19.

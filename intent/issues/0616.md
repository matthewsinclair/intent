## What

The commit gate reads a project's `guards` declaration and validates it with its own jq program, beside the binary's own reader of the same field.

- `lib/templates/hooks/pre-commit-guards.sh:173-201` reads `intent/.config/config.json` with jq and applies its own schema: `guards` must be an array, and each entry needs `run`, a non-empty array of strings, and an optional string `when`. It reports violations in its own words.
- `Config.guards: Vec<GuardDecl>` (`native/rust/crates/intentsvcs/src/project.rs:737`) parses the same field with serde, and `intent doctor` reads it (`native/rust/crates/intentsvcs/src/doctor.rs:2388`) to compare the declared guards with the hand-wired lines in a project's hooks.

Two parsers with two sets of rules for one declaration. An entry that one accepts and the other rejects leaves the commit gate and `intent doctor` disagreeing about which guards a project declares, and nothing reports the disagreement. No divergence has been measured; the defect is the second reader.

## Expected

One reader, and `Config.guards` is it. The gate takes the parsed, validated declaration from the binary, through a read verb that prints it in a form a script can read, and runs what it is given. A declaration the binary refuses is refused once, in the binary's words.

Found by cc's sweep for second copies of the binary's project checks, on hv's question of 2026-10-22. No lane yet.

A migrated inbox entry renders with the moment of the carry, not the stamp it was written with. Found in Gtools (gtools-vc, 2026-09-14): vc's five entries in cc/inbox.vc.md, written at 08:59Z, 09:06Z, 12:19Z, 12:23Z and 12:39Z (Gtools d2ac8c4), all rendered `## (2026-09-14 13:20Z)` after migrate. The entry headings are the board's only cross-node ordering, so a carried inbox loses its order.

Read against the source at v3.0.2 (e75708756). The store keeps the written stamp: `wb_migrate` passes each entry's heading stamp to `wb_insert_message` as `authored_at`. The view does not read it: `views::wb_inbox_body` renders every heading from `recorded_at`.

This needs a ruling, because D33 / AC-14.11 hold `authored_at` as untrusted text that is never read as a time (board stamps have been measured fabricated, an hour out, and ordered before the message they answer). The choice:
- render `authored_at` verbatim when present, marked as claimed, beside or instead of `recorded_at`; or
- keep rendering `recorded_at` and accept that a migrated inbox's order is lost from the view.

cc recommends the first: it shows the claimed stamp as the text it is and never parses it, which keeps D33, and it preserves what the heading exists to record.

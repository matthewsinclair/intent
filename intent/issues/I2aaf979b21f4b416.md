AE found this on 3.5.2. `intent doctor` reports root-file-behind for AGENTS.md soon after `intent init`.

Reproduced on brew 3.5.2: the cause is `intent lang init` (or `intent lang remove`) after `intent init`, not `intent init` itself. `intent init --lang <lang>` is clean. The lang verbs write the languages to `intent/.config/config.json`, and AGENTS.md lists a line for each declared language, but the lang verbs do not render AGENTS.md again.

Fix (b353): `intent lang init` and `intent lang remove` render AGENTS.md through `agents sync`'s writer when the project has one, and name it on a `wrote:` line when its bytes move.

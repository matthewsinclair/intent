## What happens

`intent use brew` refuses before removing anything unless the first `intent` on PATH after `~/.local/bin` is Homebrew's. The refusal, `SwitchError::NoBrew`, names only what would answer instead: nothing, or a copy that is not Homebrew's. It does not name the links it would have removed or the trees they serve, and it does not say why no Homebrew copy would answer: the formula is not installed, its keg is not linked, or another copy comes first on PATH. Its remedy gives `brew unpin intent && brew link intent` (or `brew install intent`) for every cause, and never says that staying on dev or opt is a choice.

- It names one cause: the formula is not installed, its keg is installed and not linked, or another copy comes first on PATH. In the last case it names that copy with its tree.
- The remedy fits the cause: install the formula, link it, or take the other copy off PATH. Each one adds staying on dev or opt.
- The check stays ahead of every removal. The cause is read only once `use brew` has refused, and from the filesystem rather than by running brew. Homebrew's prefix is the parent of the directory its `brew` is found in on PATH, as brew itself takes it. The keg is the formula's opt link, read through the tree function, so a keg with a manifest and a pre-contract keg both answer.

Seen: three fields hold the link from an agnostic principle to the language rules that concretise it:

- a language rule's `references:` (`rule-schema.md:49`);
- a pattern agnostic rule's `concretised_by:` (`rule-schema.md:50`);
- every rule's `principles:` short-names (`rule-schema.md:40`).

`rules.md:172` keeps the two link fields in step by hand. At HEAD they disagree in four ways:

- a rule sits in a principle's `concretised_by:` without citing it (`IN-EX-PHX-001` under `IN-AG-PFIC-001`);
- rules cite a principle and are missing from its list (eg `IN-SH-CODE-001` to `-004` under `IN-AG-NO-SILENT-001`);
- linked rules lack the principle's short-name in `principles:` (eg `IN-RS-CODE-002` and `highlander`);
- rules declare a short-name that no link backs in either direction (eg `IN-RS-CODE-002`'s `pfic`).

`intent claude rules validate` passes every rule. It resolves each cited id (`rules.rs:751-756`) and compares no field against another. The disagreement has readers: `in-tca-synthesize` (`SKILL.md:80`, `:122`) and `total-codebase-audit.md:601` read a principle's `concretised_by:` as exhaustive when they rank findings.

Expected: one home for the mapping, with the other fields derived from it, or `rules validate` refusing a disagreement.

The data is corrected in 3.2.1's doc audit (cc, a canon bank), on vc's ruling of 2026-09-24:

- `references:` is the home of record, because each rule's own Further Reading mirrors it.
- Each pattern rule's `concretised_by:` becomes the exact inverse of `references:`. The schema's "at least 2" is a floor, and the TCA readers already treat the list as exhaustive.
- Each language rule's `principles:` names every pattern principle the rule references.
- `IN-LU-CODE-005` gains the reference its body argues.
- `IN-RS-CODE-002` loses `pfic` and `no-silent-errors`, and `IN-SW-CODE-003` loses `pfic`; their bodies argue neither.
- `IN-EX-PHX-001` leaves `IN-AG-PFIC-001`'s list. It cites only `IN-AG-THIN-COORD-001`, and its body names that as the principle it concretises.

Left for after the stabilisation line, because each is a reading of a rule and not a data fix: seven links stand only on a `references:` entry whose rule body does not argue it:

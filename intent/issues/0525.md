Seen (ic's surface review of 0523, 2026-09-23): `intent wb edit` makes text in the shared coordination record changeable, and nothing in a render shows that an item or a message was edited. An edit made before the originating event is committed deliberately leaves no trace (0523 case 1). So a peer who read a board or an inbox live from the store, and acted on it, cannot see that the text changed afterwards. The event log cannot supply the mark, because case 1 leaves nothing in it.

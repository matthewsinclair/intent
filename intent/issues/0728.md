## What happens

`bin/.devbin/cut/gate`'s `check_rust` is a coordinator of about 50 lines that also decides inline whether its cargo reds are confined to the load-sensitive daemon family. That decision is the three-way read issue 0723 had to fix. With it buried in the coordinator, it can be exercised only by a full gate run.

## The fix

Move the decision into a pure `reds_confined <reds>` that returns 0 (every red is in the family), 1 (a red is outside it) or 2 (it could not judge). check_rust then only coordinates (PFIC), and a test can drive the three answers without cargo.

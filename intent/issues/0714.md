## What happens

`bin/.devbin/cmd/precommit` runs its parity guards from `intent/st/ST0056/parity/tools`, which is a thread's realised directory. That directory goes missing when ST0056 is dehydrated, renumbered or re-keyed, and the gate then does four things:

1. It prints one warning, "every ST0056 parity gate SKIPPED this commit (the database and whiteboard guards still ran)".
2. It sets `HAVE_TOOLS=0` and carries on.
3. Two guards after that block check for their own tool first: `guard_home_check.sh` and `shared_artefact_build_guard.sh`. Each adds "THE INSTRUMENT IS MISSING" to the gate's summary.
4. The suite-path step comes next. It runs `bash "$TOOLS/suite_path_check.sh"` without any such check, so under `set -euo pipefail` its exit 127 ends the gate before the summary prints.

`.githooks/pre-commit` passes the 127 on, so the commit is refused.

## Evidence

Driven 2026-11-19 11:01:55Z by date -u. The setup was a worktree of main at c2344d1dd, with `intent/st/ST0056` moved out (as a dehydration leaves it) and nothing staged. `bin/int precommit` exited 127. Its output, after the database step, was only:

- the warning above;
- the step headers of the guard-home, shared-build and suite-path guards;
- bash's own line, "suite_path_check.sh: No such file or directory".

It printed no "commit blocked", no list of what failed and no remedy.

## What it costs

The commit is refused, so nothing passes that should not. But the refusal is a crash, not a verdict, and it rests on an accident: the suite-path step lacks the presence check its two neighbours have. Give it one, and the gate would refuse only because those two guards happen to live in the same directory, which ST0084 means to change.

The reader also gets a false account. The gate's only line of its own says the gates were skipped and that the other guards ran, which reads as a commit going through. The two lines that name the cause are never shown. This is IN-AG-NO-SILENT-001's shape: `thread_view_skew_check.sh` retired its own fail-open on 2026-08-27, because "exit 0 is the SAME STATUS a run that examined every view returns".

## The fix

Refuse by name where the absence is found, and give `intent st hydrate ST0056` as the remedy. No step may crash the gate before its summary prints. Addressing the gate's own tools by a thread's path is ST0084's subject and stays there.

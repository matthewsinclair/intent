Measured 2026-09-13 at hv's terminal: `intent wb show vc` printed 180789 bytes, of which 169509 are the bodies of messages already marked handled, every one of them printed in full. The node had no live message at the time. `wb show` carries `--daemon` and `--json` and nothing that leaves the handled set out, and `wb pickup` renders the same list at every session start.

A status read that is mostly archive is not a status read: the live state (focus, items, holds, the messages that still need an answer) sits at the top of a dump that the terminal truncates, and the reader has to know to look only at the head. The handled messages are a record worth keeping, and they already have a home in the store; they do not belong in the default answer to "what is this node's state".

Remedy: `wb show` and `wb pickup` list live messages by default and print a one-line count of the handled ones with the verb that reaches them (`--all`, or a `wb history <node>` read); the JSON face keeps every message with its state so nothing is lost to a tool that wants the archive. Severity medium: nothing is wrong in the store, and the cost lands on the human at every status read.

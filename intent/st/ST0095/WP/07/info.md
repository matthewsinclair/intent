# WP-07: Prefix resolution

## Objective

One resolver turns what a person typed into the one record it names, or says which records it could name, and nothing stored is ever a prefix.

Seat: cc. Size: M.

## Scope

- `resolve.rs` (`:92-135`) takes a spelling and the project's ids, and returns the id, the candidates of an ambiguous prefix, or not found. It is the only place a prefix is expanded, for the CLI, the TUI and MCP.
- An ordinal-shaped spelling is always the ordinal. A hash prefix is at least 4 hex characters.
- The facade's verbs take typed ids. Claims (`facade.rs:11454`, `11477`), `events --subject` (`:7138`) and `Scope::selects` (`sync.rs:411-418`) resolve before they compare, and keep comparing whole ids.
- The pure spelling stays pure: MCP validates its arguments with no store (`mcp.rs:1915`).

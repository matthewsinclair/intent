# ST0076 AC-06.2: dc's drive on a scratch copy of Laksa

AC-06.2 is a non-test criterion: on a scratch copy of Laksa, the level-3 callers of `Map.get/2` are listed without any call to `Access.get`, `Process.get` or `Keyword.get`. vc routed the drive to a scratch copy and dc's worktree binary, because the installed pair answers no `--target` until the rebuild that follows WP-06's landing, and the copy is not the live estate.

## The copy and the binary

- **The copy.** Laksa's source, its `deps/` with their `.git` directories, and `_build/dev`, copied into dc's scratchpad on 2026-09-17. The live estate was not touched. Elixir 1.20.4 on OTP 29.
- **The binary.** `intent`, built in dc's worktree from 1a25373a6 with WP-06's bank as it then stood (patch-id c4b326db). The landing's patch differs from it only in building `dev` whatever `MIX_ENV` holds (vc, 2026-09-17), and the drive ran with `MIX_ENV` unset, so it built `dev` as well. HOME was isolated, and `MIX_HOME` and `HEX_HOME` were the user's, because a Mix project with Hex dependencies cannot compile without them.

## The resolve

`intent index resolve --lang elixir --json` ran the first, forced compile into `intent/.cache/resolve/elixir/dev` and recorded Elixir as `current`: 30,856 references matched, 17,538 unmatched and 326,308 dropped (`expansion` 316,167, `operator` 9,399, `quoted` 29 and `unread` 713), with 27 keys naming two targets. Nothing was written outside `intent/.cache`, and the copy's `_build` was not touched. A second run compiled nothing, traced nothing and stayed `current`, and the rows the first run stored stayed stored.

## The search

`intent search --target Map.get/2 --json` answered **161 rows over 148 distinct `file:line` sites**. A row is one resolved reference and a site is one line, and the two differ because a single line can write more than one call:

- 159 rows at level 3, each with target `Map.get/2`.
- 2 rows at level 2 with no target: `lib/laksa/content/linkblog.ex:338` and `lib/laksa/sites/settings_service.ex:126`. On each of those lines a written `Map.get(...)` sits beside a bracket access, which the compiler traces as `Access.get/2` under the same name, so the store holds both targets for that one key and the answer does not claim either.

A script read every hit from the answer and its line from the copy's files. Every hit's written qualifier is `Map`, and every hit's line holds `Map.get(`. **No hit is a call to `Access.get`, `Process.get` or `Keyword.get`.** Those targets resolve separately in the same store: `Keyword.get/3` on 135 rows over 135 sites, `Keyword.get/2` on **40 rows over 39 sites**, `Process.get/1` on 5 rows over 5 sites and `Access.get/2` on 2 rows over 2 sites, the last being the two keys above.

## Every figure names its unit, and why one of them did not (dc, 2026-09-17, corrected forward on the re-drive)

**This page reported `Keyword.get/2` as "on 39" beside a figure the same search answers as 40, and both numbers are right about different things.** 40 is rows; 39 is distinct `file:line` sites. The whole difference is `lib/laksa_web/plugs/unlisted_auth.ex:73`, which writes `guard(conn, Keyword.get(config, :username), Keyword.get(config, :password))` -- two calls on one line, correctly two references at one site.

**What made it invisible is that the sentence above it counts rows and calls them the same thing.** `Map.get/2`'s 161 IS a row count, over 148 sites. So one figure on this page was rows, the next was sites, and nothing on the page said which was which. `Keyword.get/3`, `Process.get/1` and `Access.get/2` read identically under both counts only because for those three the rows and the sites happen to coincide, and three agreeing figures beside one disagreeing figure is what let it through.

No number here is replaced and nothing is rewritten: 39 was never wrong, it was unlabelled. Every figure now carries its unit.

## Re-driven on the installed pair (dc, 2026-09-17)

vc's word after the rebuild: the same criterion driven again on the pair that ships, `intent 3.0.3 (58501a8a20e2d8f5106ad152cee66c86d61c45cf)`, rather than on the worktree binary this page was first written from. Every figure reproduced -- the resolve's counts by reason, the ambiguity count, the 161 rows, the 159 and 2 split, and both level-2 sites -- and all 161 hits again had their line read back out of the copy's own files rather than trusted from the answer's snippet, with none a call to `Access.get`, `Process.get` or `Keyword.get`. The binary was pinned by mtime and version string at both ends of the run, so no rebuild in the shared checkout could have swapped it mid-drive. The units defect above is the only thing the re-drive found. Banked whole at `refs/bank/dc/ac062/`, with `summary.md` as the entry point.

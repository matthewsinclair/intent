# WP-01: The lifeline: intentd exits when the owner it can observe goes away

## Objective

Bind the daemon's life to an owner it can observe, where the observation survives the owner being SIGKILLed.

## The mechanism

Whoever spawns `intentd` passes an inherited pipe; `intentd` holds the READ end and exits on EOF. **The kernel closes the write end on ANY parent death** -- clean exit, panic, SIGKILL, crashed TUI, closed terminal. No polling, no timeout to tune, no periodic `getppid`.

## Why a pipe rather than the platform's own facility

`PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` is Linux-only and this estate ships macOS first; kqueue `NOTE_EXIT` is macOS-only and needs a pid that can be recycled. **The pipe is the portable form and it is the only one with no race**: the fd either is open or is not, and no pid can be reused underneath it.

## The production path is untouched BY CONSTRUCTION

`launchd` passes no lifeline, so absent one the daemon takes the supervised branch and serves until signalled, exactly as today. **This is not a promise to be careful -- it is the default branch**, and AC-01.2 is the control that fails if it ever stops being.

# WP-08: The Desk is a hat with a written contract

## Objective

The Desk, control of a host's slots, is worn as a hat by one VC, and that is how hv has run it: devbin-vc, then webber-vc, then agentic-engineering-vc. The tool documents only the other shape, a dedicated coordinator that stands in no project. This package makes the hat a defined thing: one source for its duties, a brief the wearer is given, a board that shows who wears it, and a written answer for the day the wearer's session is gone.

## What was measured

The desk's duties are written in one place, `lib/templates/estate/_AGENTS.md`, which `intent estate init` renders for a session that "stands in no Intent project and has no whiteboard node", and whose last section says it edits no project. No skill names the desk. On 2026-11-10 the estate's control record read: took over from webber-vc, with hv's words "Asked who takes the orphaned desk, hv chose: vc takes it." So the shape in use is the one nothing describes, and each wearer has learned it from hv's prose and from what its predecessor left.

## What is built

- **One source for the duties.** What a controller does (take control, arm the watch, judge an ask against the lanes and the estate view, grant, deny, revoke, hold, release) moves to one template file. The dedicated coordinator's `AGENTS.md` and the hat's brief are both rendered from it, so the two shapes cannot drift.
- **Who may take it.** `intent estate control take` from a Claude session bound to a whiteboard node is refused unless that node is its board's vc, and the remedy says who may. `--hv "<words>"` overrides on hv's quoted word and records it, as it does today for a take under another name. A session in no project takes control as before.
- **The wearer's brief.** While a node's session holds control, its pickup prints that it wears the desk, the duties, and the asks waiting. The prompt hook already names waiting asks to a controller.
- **The board shows it.** The wearer's board header and `intent wb status` show the hat. It is read from the estate's control record at render time and stored nowhere else, so there is no second record to go stale.
- **The orphaned desk.** When the recorded controller's session is gone, `intent estate status` says the desk is orphaned and prints the step that takes it, and no pickup claims the hat.
- **How the hat sits with a VC's own work**, written into the duties: an ask that is waiting is answered before the VC's own next step; the wearer's own project books its runs through the same queue and is granted by the same judgement, on the record like any other; the hat is released at session end; and it passes to another VC on hv's word, quoted on the take.

## What it does not change

The lanes, the slots and their verbs, and the dedicated coordinator `intent estate init` renders, which stays the other legal shape.

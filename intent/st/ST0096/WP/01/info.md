# WP-01: Realisation lives in the record, and the manifest goes

## Objective

Whether a thread or an issue is realised on disk is answered by the record, by one owner, and `intent/.intentfiles` is folded into the records and removed by `intent upgrade`.

Seat: cc. Size: M. Lands after ST0095's WP-06, whose schema bump it shares, so a project migrates its canon once.

## Scope

- **cc chooses R1 or R2** (design.md, "Realisation leaves the shared manifest") and writes the choice and its reason into the design before any code (AC-01.3). R1: the record carries whether it is realised. R2: realisation is derived from status, and a record carries an override only when a person hydrated or dehydrated it against the default.
- **One owner of realisation.** One module answers whether a record is realised, and organize, hydrate, dehydrate, sync, doctor and the views ask it.
- **The verbs that change realisation write it on their own record only**: `st start`, `st hold`, `st resume`, `st done`, `st hydrate`, `st dehydrate`, `issues add` and `issues close` (the census in design.md).
- **`intent upgrade` folds `.intentfiles` into the records and removes the file**, byte-deterministically, in the same mechanical change as ST0095's format rewrite.
- **ST0095's WP-05**: whether its `.intentfiles` set merge and its regeneration of the two lists stay, cc's call (design.md, "What this changes in ST0095"), recorded in both threads, with issue 0704's disposition.

## Roster admission bar -- what an instrument must clear to join `parity/tools/`

Written down after I refused to admit dc's checker on a description. **dc's own standard was that they filed no finding against an instrument they had not read; admission runs the same way or it is not a standard.**

- **Exit 0 / 1 / 2, cannot-measure DISTINCT from a finding.**
- **States its contract and its REACH in the OUTPUT, never in a comment** -- and **a contract line is NOT self-verifying: it is another claim, and it inherits the failure mode of whatever it describes the moment a mode changes underneath it.** **AND THE SAME DEFECT LIVES IN CHECKERS, WHICH IS WIDER THAN I WROTE IT.** I scoped this to an instrument's own output; `openness.rs` validates that a declaration EXISTS and that its string starts with `carried by ` and **never looks at the referent** (vc, AC-01.7). **A checker that validates the FORM of a claim and never its REFERENT is this line pointed at someone else's claim** -- same shape as `st list`'s default filter and cc's authored `DECLARED`.

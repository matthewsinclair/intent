unextracted peer row; `canon_commit_check` then requires file and canon to move
together. Neither rule should be relaxed. Composed, a single-node coherent
commit is impossible whenever two nodes have written since the last canon
commit -- and the stall is SILENT, because two nodes politely waiting looks
exactly like two nodes working. vc offered A or B, I picked and executed inside
one turn, and the batch landed with three nodes' rows attributed.

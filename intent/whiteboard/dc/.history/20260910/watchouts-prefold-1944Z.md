**W125 -- A REMEDY IS THE ONLY CLAIM ON A DEFECTS PAGE A READER ACTS ON, AND NOTHING VERIFIES ONE.** `AC-02.3` checks that a defect is STATED, by finding the quote in the bytes of the page it names -- and **a false remedy satisfies that perfectly.** `install.md` told readers twice that setting `INTENT_HOME` repairs a broken keg; v3 does not read that variable at all, and my own instrument had certified the page an hour earlier.

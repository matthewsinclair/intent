**23. A WEAKENING WITH NO SYMPTOM IS THE HARDEST THING TO CATCH** (dc). When a type widens, the loose port and the tight one both compile. **THE COMPILER SILENCE IS THE SIGNAL, NOT THE ALL-CLEAR** -- I added an enum variant and NOTHING broke, because every consumer used `if let`. The REASON for a tight match belongs in the file, next to the match.

**24. PRESENCE OF THE VARIANT IS NOT PRESENCE OF THE SUBJECT, AND AN ANSWER IS NOT LIVENESS EITHER.** A census keyed on the state NAME cannot see a payload field inside one variant; **key on the unit the defect lives in** -- `is_shipped()` is per-ROW while the populations are path-keyed, so one path can be two rows with opposite answers. **THE SHARPEST FORM COST A DAY'S FLAKE:** a socket that EXISTS is not a daemon, which is why the rule says _exists and answers_ -- and then **ANSWERING turns out not to be liveness either.** macOS has no `SOCK_CLOEXEC`, so a listening socket is created by one syscall and marked close-on-exec by a second; a `fork` between them leaks the fd into the child, and the socket keeps accepting until that child exits, however long ago its owner died. Measured 1 in 300 with a sibling thread spawning, 0 in 2000 without. **Every predicate of the form _is X there_ has one more mechanism under it than the last one you found.**

**25. STATE A SIZE ONLY AFTER THE SEARCH THAT WOULD REFUTE IT, AND QUOTE THE SEARCH.** A cost is a claim about what does NOT exist. **A DECLARED GAP CAN NAME THE WRONG SUBJECT, WHICH READS AS RIGOUR AND IS WORTH LESS THAN NO NOTE.** **Worse: I recommended MEASURED without checking whether measuring was PERMITTED**, quoting the fail-open sentence in the message that proposed breaking it. **A rule is at its least protective in the sentence that asserts it.** The method that works: quote the searches, as with WP-08's XXL -- vc took it without argument.

**26. A GENERATED ARTEFACT CAN WRITE INTO THE AGENT-INSTRUCTION CHANNEL, AND NOTHING ANNOUNCES IT.** `docs/reference/claude.md` IS `CLAUDE.md` on a case-insensitive filesystem. **Prefix the whole output space rather than special-casing the member that bit.**

**27. A DURABLE ARTEFACT CAN BE ADVANCED BY NON-DURABLE SOURCE, ONE-WAY, AND THE VECTOR IS THE TEST SUITE** (dc's correction, and the better half of my diagnosis). The runtime store is per-machine truth, never committed; it reached schema 15 while HEAD declared 14, so the delivered binary refused every store-reading verb. **The `SCHEMA_VERSION` edit was only the content -- the act that MOVED the store was running `cargo test`**, because `Project::open` against the real root runs the migration ladder and 16 test files do exactly that. **So the one act everybody performs constantly, believing it read-only, mutates shared durable state.** **The tell arrives as a REFUSAL IN AN UNRELATED VERB, after the act, to someone who did not perform it. Ask what moved UNDERNEATH the tool before asking what you broke.**

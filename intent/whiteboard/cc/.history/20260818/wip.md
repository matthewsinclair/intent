**My worst defect of the day, and it reached hv rather than a test.** I added `seq` to `attachments` by EDITING rung 10, having reasoned: _"the live store is at 9, so rung 10 has not been applied to any durable store, so editing it in place is safe."_ **The reasoning was valid and the premise was false, and I never went and looked** -- one `PRAGMA user_version` would have said 10. Two different table shapes ended up stamped 10 with nothing able to tell them apart, and every read of canon died on `no such column: seq`.

**THE RULE, now on the ladder itself: once any store has run a rung, changing what that rung PRODUCES requires a NEW rung, never an edit to the old one.** The old rung's output is already stamped and permanently unreachable -- version-gated, and `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` would skip it anyway. It is the ladder's form of not rewriting published history. **An unchecked assumption that sounds like a check is the most expensive kind**, because it occupies the slot where the check would have gone.

### A SUITE THAT ALWAYS STARTS FRESH CANNOT SEE A MIGRATION DEFECT AT ALL

Every test builds its store from the current `DDL`, so every test gets the current shape and passes. **The defect above was reachable only from a store that existed BEFORE the change -- ours, and any real user's.** It is `a subject that cannot exhibit the defect cannot clear it`, arriving in the one place where it costs a user rather than us.
